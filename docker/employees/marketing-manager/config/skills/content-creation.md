# Content Creation

Write high-quality marketing content in Norwegian Bokmål or English depending on audience.

## Outputs
- Blog posts (800–2000 words, SEO-aware, structured with H2/H3)
- LinkedIn posts (short-form, hook + insight + CTA, max 1300 characters)
- Press releases (inverted pyramid structure, quote from leadership)
- Case studies (problem / solution / result format)
- Email newsletters (subject line + preview text + body + CTA)

## Standards
- Active voice, short sentences
- Data-backed claims where possible
- Brand tone: professional, direct, human-centred
- Always store the draft as an artefact before requesting approval
