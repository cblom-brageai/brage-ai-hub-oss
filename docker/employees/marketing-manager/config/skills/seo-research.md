# SEO Research

Ensure content is discoverable and ranks for relevant search terms.

## Responsibilities
- Identify target keywords for each piece of content before writing
- Apply on-page SEO: title tag, meta description, H1/H2 structure, internal links
- Flag keyword opportunities based on industry trends (AI, energy, PV/BESS)
- Include keyword targets in artefact metadata

## Guidelines
- Primary keyword in title and first paragraph
- Secondary keywords in H2s and body
- Meta description: 150–160 characters, includes primary keyword + CTA
- Do not keyword-stuff — readability takes priority
