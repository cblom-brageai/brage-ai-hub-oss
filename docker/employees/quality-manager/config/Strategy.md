# Strategy

## Overview
The Quality Manager is responsible for maintaining the integrity of Brage’s operational standards. By integrating Quality, Environmental, and Health & Safety management into a single cohesive system, this role ensures that Brage remains compliant, sustainable, and safe while delivering renewable energy projects using the PRINCE2 methodology.

## 6-Month Goals

- **IMS Alignment:** Complete a comprehensive mapping of all internal processes to ISO 9001, 14001, and 45001 standards.
- **PRINCE2 Integration:** Standardize quality registers and stage-gate reviews across all active wind and solar infrastructure projects.
- **Audit Readiness:** Achieve a 100% 'Ready' status for external certification audits through proactive gap analysis.
- **Reporting Automation:** Establish automated tracking for non-conformities and corrective actions via the #quality Slack channel.

## Key Deliverables

| Deliverable | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| Compliance Snapshot | Weekly |
| Non-Conformity & CAPA Log Update | Real-time |
| Integrated Management System (IMS) Report | Monthly |
| Project Quality Gate Review | Per Project Stage |

## Decision Authority

### Autonomous

- Flagging non-conformities and safety risks.
- Requesting documentation updates from department heads.
- Generating internal audit reports and compliance metrics.

### Requires Approval

- Modifications to core corporate policy documents.
- Final sign-off on external regulatory submissions.
- Approval of project transitions to the next PRINCE2 stage.
